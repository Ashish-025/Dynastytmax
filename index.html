What does Dynasty Max do?

We cover three areas for businesses: vehicle solutions (GPS tracking, AIS-140 VLTD, mining VTS, fuel sensors, dash cameras, speed governors and fire safety), security and infrastructure (CCTV, solar, networking, biometrics and access control) and digital solutions (websites, software, branding, graphic design, SEO, digital marketing and print).

Are your GPS devices AIS-140 approved?

Yes. We supply AIS-140 / ICAT-approved trackers for commercial vehicles, so they are ready for RTO passing. See the devices on our vehicle solutions page.

Can you help with mining vehicle tracking and i3MS?

Yes. We fit mining VTS for vehicles that need to be registered on i3MS. You can check a vehicle’s status on the i3MS portal with the Get Started button at the top of the page.

Do you install and support what you sell?

Yes. Our own team plans, installs and looks after every job, and we handle AMC, renewals and service requests after the work is done.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Odisha and work with businesses across the state. Tell us where your vehicles or sites are and we will let you know how we can help.

How do I get a quote?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or fill in the form on our contact page with what you need, and we will get back to you with options and a price.
