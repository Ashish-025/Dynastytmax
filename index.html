Vehicle tracking

What is AIS-140, and does your vehicle need it?

AIS-140 is India’s standard for tracking public and commercial vehicles, with live GPS and an emergency button. We supply and fit approved devices.

 Mining

How to check a mining vehicle’s status on i3MS

Vehicles carrying minerals in Odisha are registered on the i3MS portal. Open the registered vehicle report and search by vehicle number.

 Security

Choosing CCTV for your office or site

Start with the areas that matter most: entrances, cash counters and parking. Then decide how long to keep recordings and who should see them live.
