Privacy Policy

How Dynasty Max handles your information when you visit this website or contact us.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

On this page

Who we are Information we collect How we use it Sharing Cookies & storage Third-party services How long we keep it Your rights Security Children Changes Contact & grievances

1. Who we are

This website, dynastymax.in, is run by Dynasty Max (“we”, “us”), which provides vehicle tracking, security and surveillance, and digital and IT solutions across Odisha, with offices in Chandikhol, Cuttack, Bhubaneswar and Puri. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices you have. It is written to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to send us. This website has no accounts and does not store form entries. When you use the contact form, the question box or any “WhatsApp” button, your message (and any name, email, phone number or subject you type) is passed to WhatsApp on your own device, and you decide whether to send it. When you call or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and what you tell us.

Information about our customers’ services. When you buy a service from us, such as a GPS tracker, AIS-140 device, CCTV system or website, we may hold details needed to provide and support it, for example your name, contact details, address, vehicle numbers and device details. Information produced by tracking or camera systems is handled under the agreement for that service.

Technical information. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical details of each visit, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the date and time, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries, prepare quotes and arrange site visits.
  • To supply, install, renew and support the products and services you buy from us, including AMC and service requests.
  • To meet legal and regulatory requirements, for example for AIS-140 devices or mining vehicles registered on i3MS.
  • To keep this website working and secure.

We use your information with your consent (for example, when you send us a message) or where it is needed to provide a service you have asked for or to meet a legal obligation. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing your information

We share information only when needed: with the manufacturers, platforms and partners that make a product you buy work (for example, a tracking platform or a government portal where registration is required), with service providers who help us run our business, or when the law requires it.

5. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set cookies and does not track you across other websites. It only removes an old display setting (“dm-theme”) from your browser’s storage, if one was saved there on an earlier visit.

6. Third-party services

Some parts of this website rely on, or link to, other services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Website hosting (Vercel), which serves the pages and keeps the technical logs described above.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces; your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • WhatsApp, when you choose to message us there.
  • Links to the i3MS portal, Google Maps, app stores and our social media pages. When you follow these links, the other website’s privacy policy applies.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages and customer records only as long as needed to respond, provide and support our services, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. After that, we delete them or make them anonymous.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it, where we no longer need to keep it;
  • withdraw a consent you have given (this does not affect anything done before you withdraw).

You can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. To make a request, contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

This website is served only over a secure HTTPS connection with strict security settings. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

10. Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
